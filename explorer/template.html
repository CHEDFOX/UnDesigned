Background
Colour names and CMYK values follow the Seigensha edition of Sanzo Wada's book. Screen colours are converted from CMYK and will differ slightly from print. Roles: bg is the lightest (or darkest) colour, ink the colour with the most contrast against it, accent the most saturated of the rest. When ink falls below 4.5:1, the badge marks it and text falls back to the book's Black or White.
