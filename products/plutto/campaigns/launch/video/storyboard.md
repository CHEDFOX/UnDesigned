Checks:
- Beat 5 would need 2.38 s and the 15 s cut has only 1.5 s for it, so it is **cut from the 15 s** and that time goes to the end card; it stays in a 30 s cut.
- One thing moves at a time in every beat; the orb never moves after it lands (NS8), only things arrive around it.
- Text sits in the live band (top 14%, bottom 35%, sides 10%); the orb sits outside the text zone.
- The template card runs 10 s on its own (orb 0–1.8 s, headline 1.8 s, brand 4.9 s, CTA 6.1 s, still from 7.3 s). In the 15 s cut the orb is already on screen, so the card's text beats take 10.5–15.0 s (headline reading hold 3.1 s, brand and CTA 1.2 s each), ending on a short still; for a longer final hold, run the card as the 10 s stand-alone version.

## 6. Accessibility and safety checklist

- [x] No flashes: every element fades in once; the ground never changes.
- [x] Reduced-motion version: final frame only.
- [x] Nothing moves for more than 5 s; the story ends on a still.
- [ ] Captions: only needed if a voice-over is recorded.
- [x] Key visual information is also in words.
- [x] Text contrast 18.6:1 (white on Wada Black) on every frame.
- [x] Text, logo and CTA inside the 9:16 safe area at every frame.
