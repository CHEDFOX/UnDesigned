# Creative brief

**Campaign:** Quiet mornings (Negative Space posters)
**Date / deadline:** —
**Owner:** Rye & Rise (SAMPLE: invented brand)

**Style for this campaign:** Negative Space (`approaches/negative-space`), chosen by the user for these posters. The product's own direction stays Humanist Minimal; colours and type stay the product's (palette 344 primary, pairing young-onest, which Negative Space rates "good"). No hand face: Negative Space uses none.

## 1. Research (O1)

- What the product is and how it actually works: slow-fermented sourdough (36 hours), baked every night, ready on a named shelf from 6:30 on weekdays; pre-order by 9 pm.
- What customers say about it, in their own words: (from messaging.json) 4.9 stars from 600+ neighbours.
- What competitors say: supermarkets sell fast, soft bread; artisan bakeries open late at weekends. We must not sound like either: no "artisanal", no "world-class".

## 2. Audience (P2, S2)

- The one person this is for: a neighbour who leaves for work before most bakeries open.
- What they want: good bread on a weekday without planning the morning around it.

## 3. Problem (S3)

- External: bakeries open too late and sell out early.
- Internal: settling for second-best food on weekdays.
- Philosophical: good bread shouldn't be a weekend luxury.

## 4. The one thing (W1)

> Your good loaf is already waiting for you at 6:30.

## 5. Dramatic truth and proof (W2, O5, S4)

- The honest fact: the bread takes 36 hours and a whole night to make, so it is ready before you are.
- Proof: 36-hour fermentation, baked every night since 2019, a shelf with your name, 4.9 stars from 600+ neighbours.

## 6. Big idea (O2, W8)

> The empty page is the quiet of the early morning: one small loaf alone in a calm field, already made, already yours.

Each poster gives the space one meaning:
1. **Time** (`ns-poster-object-low`): a small loaf far off on a long horizon. The space is the 36 hours.
2. **Reserved** (`ns-poster-corner-type`): one loaf on a short shelf with a small blue name tag. The space is the empty shop, kept for you.
3. **Night** (`ns-poster-object-low`, mirrored idea): a small loaf with one thin line of steam rising into the empty top. The space is the night it was baked in.

## 7. Call to action (S6)

- Direct: Pre-order tonight's loaf
- Transitional: Get the weekly bake list (not on posters)
- Long term (P4): pre-order accounts

## 8. Stakes and success (S7)

- If they do nothing: another rushed breakfast of supermarket toast.
- After they act: real bread before work, a little ahead of the day.

## 11. Check before sign-off

- [x] Grunt test: real bread, ready at 6:30, pre-order by 9 pm
- [x] One message only (W1)
- [x] Customer is the hero ("your", "you")
- [x] Brand name directly under the headline (O4)
- [x] Words and image add to each other: the image never shows what the headline says
- [x] Still makes sense in five years (P3)

- [x] Colours from palette 344 only; dark ink on a light ground (O8)

## 12. How we'll measure it (O10, P5)

- Success metric: pre-orders per week from the north side.
- What we'll test: "Thirty-six hours" vs "This one has your name on it".
- Evergreen version after launch: "Reserved" poster as the shop-window poster.
