# Creative brief: Say it badly. Send it perfect.

> **Rendered by the guide.** `npm run design -- tailzu say-it-badly --png`. Style, palette, pairing and hand face are Tailzu's own (`brand.json`).

- **Core (identity.json):** people think in speech but are made to perform in type. Tailzu keeps your voice and drops only the noise: it cleans, it never rewrites.
- **Pillar:** Said → Written.
- **One message (W1):** Say it the way it comes; Tailzu sends it clean, in your own words.
- **Big idea (O2):** one proof sheet. The said line in your hand, with the fillers struck out the way an editor marks a proof; the clean line lands in a message bubble, with the number kept and the amber full stop last. Two everyday symbols (a proof mark and a chat bubble) make one idea, per Humanist Minimal's metaphor recipe.
- **Headline:** "Say it badly. Send it perfect." The result line of the brand's own one-liner (messaging.json); the customer is the hero ("you").
- **The example:** said "umm kal like 7 baje" → written "Kal 7 baje." Hinglish stays Hinglish, the 7 stays exactly as said and is underlined (kept, not coloured), only "umm" and "like" go. An illustration of what the product does, not a customer quote.
- **Style:** Humanist Minimal. Ink line and white cut paper on a flat ground, one accent object (the amber full stop), more than 40% empty ground, sentence case, three type sizes plus one hand note of five words.
- **Colour:** Tailzu desk, Wada 190 dark (picked over primary 126 at the user's request): Black ground (the app's own near-black), Ivory Buff text 12.75:1 (the mark's cream), English Red accent 5.22:1 on the full stop only (the copper of the mark's tail).
- **Type:** pairing `instrument`: Instrument Serif for the headline and the written line (calm type), Instrument Sans for the button; Caveat for the said line (the brand's own hand for speech, Schroll et al. 2018). Names and numbers stay in the serif (Song & Schwarz 2008).
- **Brand:** no logo on this piece (the user asked for it removed, `brand: false`); the name stays in the call to action.
- **Motion (video.json, motion.json):** one mover at a time over a 10 s cycle: the hand line writes in (1.3 s), each filler is struck (0.6 s each), the bubble springs up, the clean line settles, the amber dot drops in with a spring, then 4.5 s still. No flashes. Reduced motion, and the PNG, show the end frame.
- **CTA:** "Download Tailzu free at tailzu.space" (the brandscript's direct call to action).
- **Copy check:** 0 errors, 0 warnings.
- **Files:** `poster.copy.json`; art `products/tailzu/art.mjs` → `proof-slip`; output `designs/poster-say-it-badly.svg` (animated) and `.png`.
