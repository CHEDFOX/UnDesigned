Brand hubUnDesigned Design System

Everything the marketing is built from: the colour system, the messaging playbook, a copy checker and the brand message. It is generated from the repository, so it always matches the files designers use.

How a campaign is made

Six stages, in order. The first two are done once for the brand; the rest happen for every campaign. Rule codes point to the messaging playbook.

    Colour

    Sanzo Wada's A Dictionary of Color Combinations (1933): colours in families, arranged into combinations. Every piece uses one combination. Star the ones you like and copy your shortlist to choose the brand palettes.

    Colours
    Background
    shown

    Colour names and CMYK values follow the Seigensha edition of Wada's book; screen colours are converted from CMYK and differ slightly from print. Roles: bg is the lightest (or darkest) colour, ink the one with most contrast against it, accent the most saturated of the rest. When ink falls below 4.5:1 the badge says so and text falls back to the book's Black or White.

    Messaging playbook

    Four books, each with one job in the process. Rules are paraphrased and coded by book (P, S, O, W) so feedback can point at them. Rules marked auto-checked are tested by the copy checker.

    Rules

    Copy limits by format

    Most words allowed in each part; 0 means leave it out. Rules of thumb, editable in foundations/messaging/source/formats.json.

    Copy checker

    Type the words for one piece and see it laid out in the brand palette, checked against the playbook as you type. It is the same checker as npm run check:copy. It catches mechanical problems; whether the idea is good is still a human call (W1, W2, O2).
